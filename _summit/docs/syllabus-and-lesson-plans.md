## Submissions

Use one personal Google Sheet and one personal Colab notebook all year. For each assignment, open its Google Form, use your school account, type your answers, paste the Python code and its results where requested, and include your work link. Paste the full letter into its Form. Press Submit; the Google Forms confirmation is your receipt. Questions go through the separate check-in Form. Resubmit after revisions; teachers review the latest response.

All student work is private in Google Drive. The public website has no student login or completion status.
