North Star Summit 2026-27: Data Science

Syllabus and schedule · Mr. Hayward · george@haywarddatascience.com
Grade 12 · About six students · Mr. Hayward and two school teachers

All dates are tentative until the school confirms them. Meeting times will be set in Eastern time. We meet about once a month in the fall and about every three weeks from January to March. Most work happens between meetings. We start on October 22, finish Sheets before winter break, start Python in mid-January and present in mid-April.

How it works: Each assignment follows the same pattern: we introduce it at a meeting, you send questions, you turn in your work, and Mr. Hayward sends feedback. School breaks can stretch a cycle.

How to submit: Use one personal Google Sheet and one personal Colab notebook all year. For each assignment, open its Google Form while signed in to your school account. Type your answers, paste your code and results where asked, and include the link to your work. Paste the full letter into its Form. The Google Forms confirmation is your receipt. Send questions through the check-in Form. You can resubmit after revisions, and teachers review your latest response.

Breaks: No required course work from December 11 to January 13. We also pause for assessments on October 26 to 30 and January 19 to 22, and for assessments and spring break from March 22 to April 2. Dates avoid school closures and events on the school calendar.
