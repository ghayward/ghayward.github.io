How to turn in your work

  1. Work in your own Google Sheet or Colab notebook.
  2. Open the answer Form in the lesson. Use your school Google account.
  3. Type your answers. For coding questions, paste your code and its result. Add your file link.
  4. Press Submit in Google Forms and look for its confirmation page.

This public website has no login or student account. Google handles your file permissions and the Form’s sign-in. Your teachers receive a timestamped response; this page does not display a completion status.

The syllabus

October to April · Grade 12 · About six students

We’ll use the same real dataset to learn spreadsheets, Python and clear communication. Then we’ll ask AI to help us—and check whether its answers deserve our trust.

How class works

Mr. Hayward leads monthly fall workshops, then calls about every three weeks from January through March. Most work happens asynchronously with support from two school teachers. Each cycle has a questions deadline, a submission deadline and a feedback date. Plan on roughly 30–60 minutes in an active week; your teachers can adjust this with you.

For each assignment, work in your own files, then open its Google Form. Sign in with your school Google account when the Form asks. Type your answers, paste the code and its result for coding questions, and include your work link. You can revise your files and submit again. Feedback focuses on showing your work, getting the evidence right, explaining your thinking and making improvements.
