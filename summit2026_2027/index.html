The syllabus

October to April · Grade 12 · About six students

We’ll use the same real dataset to learn spreadsheets, Python and clear communication. Then we’ll ask AI to help us—and check whether its answers deserve our trust.

How class works

Mr. Hayward leads one live workshop each month. Between workshops, you work asynchronously with support from two school teachers. Plan on roughly 30–60 minutes in an active week; your teachers can adjust this with you.

For each assignment, work in your own files, then open its Google Form. Type your answers and paste your work link. You can revise your files and submit again. Feedback focuses on showing your work, getting the evidence right, explaining your thinking and making improvements.
